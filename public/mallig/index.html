Mallig EP (2021)

by Purpo
Produced by: Purpo, Chuck Joson, Jem Blanco
Mallig is a collection of deeply personal songs written at different points in my life. Rooted in the alternative OPM sound I grew up with, the EP moves through stories and struggles with freedom, love, expectations, and the passage of time. A lot of these songs came from moments when life seemed to be moving faster than I could make sense of it. Looking back, writing them might’ve just been my way of finding some meaning in the chaos.
