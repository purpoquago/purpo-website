Mallig EP (2021)

by Purpo
Produced by: Purpo, Chuck Joson, Jem Blanco
Mallig is a collection of deeply personal songs written at different points in my life. Rooted in the alternative OPM sound I grew up with, the EP moves through stories of freedom, love, expectations, and the passage of time. A lot of these songs came from moments when life seemed to be moving faster than I could make sense of it. At its heart, Mallig is about moving forward while still carrying pieces of where I came from.

Mallig Side B EP (2022)

by Purpo
Produced by: Purpo, Jem Blanco
Mallig Side B is a collection of stories I gathered from the new places and people I encountered along the way. Most of them aren’t really my stories, but stories of and for people I loved and have come to love. In one way or another, they explore different kinds of love whether for a person, or an idea.
